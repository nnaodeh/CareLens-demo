Manager workflow

Every CareLens finding links to source evidence. Managers can accept, reject, assign, or resolve findings. CareLens does not alter the underlying care record.

Upload care records

This public manager demo uses synthetic data only. A production CareLens service could accept exported PDF, Word, Excel or CSV records after the required security and UK GDPR controls are in place. Production integrations can come later; the first goal is to prove the analysis and manager-review workflow.
